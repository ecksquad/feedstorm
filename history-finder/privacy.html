Feedstorm: Find My Sites — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-26

Feedstorm: Find My Sites is a browser extension with a single purpose: reading your browsing history, on your device, to suggest sites you might want to follow in Feedstorm — a separate news reader website. This page explains exactly what the extension does with your data, in full.

What data it accesses

Your browsing history, via the browser's history API — and nothing else. The extension requests no other permission.

When it accesses it

Only after you open the extension's popup and explicitly click "Show my top sites" on the consent screen shown first. Installing the extension does not, by itself, cause your history to be read. Clicking "Not now," or just closing the popup without clicking "Show my top sites," means your history is never read at all.

Where that data goes

Nowhere but your own screen. Specifically:

  • The extension has no server, no analytics, and makes no network requests of any kind. It cannot send your history anywhere even if it wanted to.
  • It does not request the storage permission and never calls chrome.storage — it cannot save anything to disk.
  • The ranked list of sites it builds exists only in memory, inside the popup document. A browser extension popup is destroyed the instant it closes; nothing persists between openings.
  • The only data that ever leaves the popup is the specific site domains you tick and confirm by clicking "Add to Feedstorm" — and even then, it only travels as far as the address bar of a new browser tab opened on your own machine, pointed at Feedstorm's website. Nothing crosses the network.

What you see but don't pick

Any site the scan finds that you don't tick is discarded the moment the popup closes, with no exception. The list itself is not filtered by what a site is about — every site you've actually visited is shown the same way, ranked only by how often you've been there. You decide what to keep by ticking it, not by anything being hidden from you or pre-judged on your behalf.

Third parties

None. No analytics providers, no advertising networks, no data brokers. The source code is public: github.com/ecksquad/feedstorm/tree/main/history-finder.
