⚡ Feedstorm · Find my sites

To suggest sites, Feedstorm needs to look at your browsing history for a moment - here's exactly what that does and doesn't mean.

  • Nothing is recorded. The list this builds only ever exists on this screen, in this popup's memory - it's gone the instant you close this window, whether you pick anything or not.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere. This extension has no server, makes no network requests, and can't save to disk even if it wanted to - it only reads your history, right here, right now.
  • Only what you tick goes anywhere at all - and even then, only as far as opening Feedstorm in a new tab on your own machine.
  • The sites you do add can't see or store anything about you. Feedstorm fetches their latest posts through its own relay, so the sites themselves never see your device - they just hand over their public latest headlines, same as anyone else's request.
